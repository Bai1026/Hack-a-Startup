[POL-p1-L1] 1. Purpose
[POL-p1-L2] This policy describes when Alderpoint Health Plan covers skilled home health services.
[POL-p1-L3] 2. Definitions
[POL-p1-L4] Homebound: leaving home requires a considerable and taxing effort, and the member needs the help of
[POL-p1-L5] another person or a supportive device to leave home. Absences for medical care do not by themselves
[POL-p1-L6] change homebound status.
[POL-p1-L7] Skilled service: a service that requires the skills of a registered nurse or a licensed therapist to be
[POL-p1-L8] delivered safely and effectively.
[POL-p1-L9] Facility-based alternative: admission to a skilled nursing facility or an inpatient rehabilitation facility.
[POL-p1-L10] 3. Coverage criteria
[POL-p1-L11] 3.1 Standard criteria. Home health services are medically necessary when all of the following are met:
[POL-p1-L12] (a) A physician ordered the services under a written plan of care after a face-to-face encounter related
[POL-p1-L13] to the reason for home health.
[POL-p1-L14] (b) The member needs intermittent skilled nursing or skilled therapy services.
[POL-p1-L15] (c) The treating practitioner certified in the record that the member is homebound, with a statement of
[POL-p1-L16] the clinical findings that support homebound status.
[POL-p1-L17] 3.2 Visit limits. Up to 60 visits per benefit year across all disciplines. Additional visits require a new review.
[POL-p1-L18] 3.3 Home-based care in place of a facility admission. Home health services ordered in place of a
[POL-p1-L19] facility-based alternative are medically necessary, without certification of homebound status under 3.1(c),
[POL-p1-L20] when all of the following are documented:
[POL-p1-L21] (a) The member meets Plan criteria for admission to a skilled nursing facility or an inpatient
[POL-p1-L22] rehabilitation facility, as documented by the treating physician or a case manager.
[POL-p1-L23] (b) The home setting was evaluated and found clinically appropriate and safe for the ordered services,
[POL-p1-L24] including caregiver availability when a caregiver is needed.
[POL-p1-L25] (c) The expected cost of the home health services does not exceed the expected cost of the
[POL-p1-L26] facility-based alternative.
[POL-p1-L27] 4. Documentation
[POL-p1-L28] Acceptable documentation includes the plan of care, the face-to-face encounter note, therapy evaluations,
[POL-p1-L29] case management and discharge planning notes, and home safety evaluations.
[POL-p1-L30] 5. Not covered
[POL-p1-L31] Custodial care, homemaker services, and services that a caregiver without clinical training can provide
[POL-p1-L32] safely.
[POL-p1-L33] 6. Review of this policy
[POL-p1-L34] This policy is reviewed annually. The version in force on the date of the determination applies.
